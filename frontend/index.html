Selected Supreme Court precedents

Short urban-planning enforcement references for policy and legislative review. These judgments are not interpretations of the BNS.

  1. Friends Colony Development Committee v. State of Orissa, (2004) 8 SCC 733

    Emphasized planned development and public safety; deliberate, non-compoundable deviations by professional builders should not be routinely regularized, and accountability of responsible officials may also require attention. The matter was remitted for fact-specific determination.

  2. Esha Ekta Apartments Co-operative Housing Society Ltd. v. Municipal Corporation of Mumbai, (2013) 5 SCC 357

    Rejected regularization of the disputed unauthorized floors on those facts, allowed the municipal notices to be implemented, and left purchasers free to pursue appropriate remedies against the builders.

  3. Dipak Kumar Mukherjee v. Kolkata Municipal Corporation, (2013) 5 SCC 336

    For admitted construction beyond the sanctioned plan despite stop-work notices, directed demolition, buyer refunds with interest, and costs. The order turned on the evidence and statutory framework in that case.

  4. Supertech Ltd. v. Emerald Court Owner Resident Welfare Association, (2021) 10 SCC 1

    Upheld demolition of two towers after finding specific building-regulation and apartment-law violations; directed the developer to bear demolition costs and refund affected purchasers. It is a fact-specific judgment, not an automatic remedy for every planning flag.

Check the full judgments, later decisions, and current state and municipal statutes before relying on a precedent in drafting or enforcement.
